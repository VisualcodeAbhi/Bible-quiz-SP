Telugu Bible Quiz is an educational and Christian trivia quiz application developed by the Agape Rhythm Team. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver and sync your gaming experience:

  • Account & Authentication Data: If you sign in via Google Sign-In or Email, we receive your email address, display name, and avatar URL to identify your account and sync your game progress across devices.
  • Game Progress Data: Your quiz scores, completed chapters, lives, and hints are securely stored on our cloud database (Supabase) to prevent data loss.
  • Guest Mode: If you play as a Guest, all progress is stored locally on your device without transmitting personal identifiers.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our app utilizes trusted third-party service providers that may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In: For authentication and secure access.
  • Google AdMob: To display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. AdMob may use device advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy to serve relevant ads.
  • Supabase: For cloud database storage, real-time sync, and user account authentication.

3. Data Security & Storage

We value your trust in providing us your information. We implement industry-standard Row Level Security (RLS) and SSL/TLS encrypted connections to protect your personal information and game progress.

4. Account and Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data. You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time directly within the app by going to Settings / Profile → Delete Account. When requested, your user account and all associated game records are permanently deleted from our servers immediately.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app contains general biblical educational quiz content suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 without parental consent.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.
